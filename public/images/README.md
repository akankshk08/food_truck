# Photography

Drop photos into this folder using the file names below (any of `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.avif`),
then rebuild/redeploy. Each one automatically replaces its placeholder — no code changes needed.
Next.js resizes and compresses them for fast loading, so upload the best-quality original you have
(ideally 2000–3000px on the long edge).

| File name            | Where it appears        | Shot brief |
|----------------------|-------------------------|-----------|
| `hero-food-truck`    | Hero (full screen)      | Wide landscape. Dark-green food truck at night, warm golden lighting, palm trees / tropical foliage. Keep the left third darker — the headline sits there. |
| `chicken-sukka`      | Menu card 1             | **Authentic Mangalorean dry Chicken Sukka.** Bite-sized chicken pieces, thick dark reddish-brown roasted masala, visible roasted grated coconut, curry leaves, caramelised onions. Dry / semi-dry — very little gravy. Brass bowl or banana-leaf-lined plate. **Not** curry, butter chicken or any gravy dish. |
| `kori-rotti`         | Menu card 2             | Brass bowl of rich reddish-brown coastal chicken gravy, with separate crisp, thin kori rotti alongside. Banana-leaf presentation. |
| `mushroom-biryani`   | Menu card 3             | Mushroom biryani in a brass handi: visible mushroom pieces, long-grain basmati, fried onions, coriander and mint. |
| `story-cooking`      | Our Story (large, portrait 4:5) | Hands roasting masala in an iron kadai over flame, steam, warm light. |
| `story-spices`       | Our Story (square)      | Overhead: Byadgi chillies, coriander, pepper, cumin, fresh grated coconut on stone or brass. |
| `story-coconut`      | Our Story (small, portrait 3:4) | Close-up: freshly grated coconut and curry leaves beside a grinding stone. |

Menu card photos are cropped to roughly 4:3 — keep the dish centred.
Alt text for every photo is set in `config/brand.ts`.
